Why the navigation is designed this way

Clear labels, short descriptions, and grouped links help visitors understand what they will find before they click. Together these strengthen information scent: the cues visitors use to decide which path is most likely to meet their needs.

Sources

  • Information Scent: How Users Decide Where to Go Next Supports clear labels and descriptions that add useful detail about destinations. nngroup.com/articles/information-scent
  • Mega Menus Work Well for Site Navigation Supports grouping related options under descriptive headings to help users understand their choices. nngroup.com/articles/mega-menus-work-well
