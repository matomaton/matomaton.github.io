Who can fix what

Some of this is a design problem and some of it is not. The sprint can own the first column. The second needs an engineering commitment.

The sprint can design for

  • Skeleton and progressive states so owners know whether the page is finished
  • Stable layout so availability does not shift while owners scan (search had the only clearly poor layout shift, CLS 0.31)
  • Disabling primary actions until data is ready
  • Fewer elements competing for first paint on search and reservation
  • Always show a loading status; never animate completion without a next screen; scroll new results into view (Trust walkthrough, Oct 15)

Only engineering can fix

  • Server response time before the HTML arrives (reservation ~4 to 6 s, resort ~2 to 5 s)
  • Heavy booking and toolkit bundles (booking.js ~2 MB, 5 to 6 s)
  • Oversized original PNGs from the CMS (up to 3.5 MB each)
  • Chatbot, A/B testing, and tag scripts loading before results are visible

Questions for the engineering team

Ordered by leverage. The first two are enough to start a deeper dig.

  1. Why does the reservation page wait 4 to 6 seconds before any HTML returns? The same question applies to the resort page (about 5 s). We need a document-only trace, not another full test.
  2. Is booking.js required at first paint on search? Search is the worst page overall (score 0.31) and carries about 1.5 MB of unminified script.
  3. Can search return stable placeholders and progressive results instead of a blank or shifting grid?
  4. Which templates request original-size images, and can we serve sized WebP or AVIF instead?
  5. Can chat, surveys, and experiments wait until after results are visible?
  6. Can we agree a shared target, for example "search results usable in under 3 s on a typical member session"?
