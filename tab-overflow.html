Pattern A + overflow — scaling to many tabs

The active tab always shows its full label. Inactive tabs shrink to a comfortable floor, then to icon-only (hover for the name). When even icon-only tabs won't fit, the overflow spills into a More ▾ menu with a live count. Drag the width down or push tabs to 20 to see all three stages.
