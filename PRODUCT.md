# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS (no framework, no build step)

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Matthew Martin for principal product design roles. They arrive with limited time and are scanning for seniority signals, breadth of experience, and craft quality rather than reading every case study in depth. Secondary: potential clients considering him for freelance/contract design work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and resume site whose job is to get Matthew Martin considered — and ideally interviewed or hired — for principal product design roles (or client engagements) by demonstrating end-to-end product design skill through real case studies, a resume, and supporting articles.

## Positioning

20 years designing end-to-end product experiences, design systems, and AI-assisted tools, across domains rather than a single vertical, now targeting principal-level roles. AI-assisted tooling and design systems are the current specialty and throughline across recent case studies (e.g. ThreeLevelsofAIOutput, artifactSelection, project-contentLoader, recommendationAlignment). Enterprise software for cultural institutions (referenced on the homepage) is one client vertical/case-study example among several, not the whole positioning.

## Operating Context

Visitors reach the site directly (personal domain) or via a resume/LinkedIn link, land on the homepage or resume.html, and from there branch into case-study pages (e.g. BeyondClicks, appMerg, artifactSelection, designGovernance, frictionGap, recommendationAlignment, schedulingConflicts, testingMethodology, tripPlaner) or the articles section. A downloadable resume PDF is also offered directly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static site (GitHub Pages-style hosting, no backend). No CMS — each page is hand-authored HTML. Multiple legacy/parallel stylesheets and token files currently coexist (stylesgem.css is canonical/current; styles.css and colors_and_type.css appear to be earlier iterations not linked from the homepage) — undecided whether/when to consolidate or remove the legacy files.

## Evidence on Hand

- Case-study pages under the repo root (BeyondClicks.html, ThreeLevelsofAIOutput.html, appMerg.html, artifactSelection.html, designGovernance.html, frictionGap.html, project-contentLoader.html, recommendationAlignment.html, schedulingConflicts.html, testingMethodology.html, tripPlaner.html, plus projects.html and articles.html as index/listing pages).
- resume.html plus downloadable resume PDFs at the repo root.
- No testimonials, press, or third-party proof currently on the site — do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

- Fast, credible first impression over exhaustive detail — recruiters skim.
- Breadth (20 years, multiple domains) paired with a clear current specialty (AI-assisted tools, design systems), not diluted into a generic generalist message.
- Site should read as principal-level: strategic framing, systems thinking, and outcomes — not just execution craft.
- Every case study should read as genuinely authored by this designer, not a template a recruiter has seen from ten other candidates.
- Resume and case studies must stay factually consistent with each other (no claims in one page contradicted by another).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific standard confirmed yet; treat WCAG AA as the working baseline for a public-facing professional site until told otherwise.
