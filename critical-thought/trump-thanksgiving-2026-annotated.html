About the screenshots

Real debates sit underneath: GDP revisions can be large and trust in data matters; Somali Minnesotans do have high poverty and household benefit-use rates; the White House needs upkeep; removing violent offenders is a legitimate goal. Takeaway

When a post lists many enemies at once, check each claim on its own. Bundling lets the weakest claims (Sharia, “2%”) borrow credibility from the one that's true (the GDP revision happened).
