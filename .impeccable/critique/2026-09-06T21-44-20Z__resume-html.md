## Design Specificity Verdict

**Split verdict — bespoke skin, generic/stale content.** The visual frame (masthead type, `.resume-block-title` hairline rhythm, the shared article-header system) is genuinely this designer's own system. But the content undercuts it: the summary paragraph is interchangeable boilerplate, Core Competencies/Technical Skills are flat 22- and 18-item laundry lists, and — most damaging — **the page's own headline claim contradicts the rest of the site.**

**Deterministic scan** (`impeccable detect --json resume.html`, 3 findings, all confirmed true positives): a continuous uppercase-styled contact line (`.label` on the address/phone/email/site/LinkedIn row) plus 12 uppercased `.resume-item-date` spans — real content, not short labels, uppercased; the same shared-nav `2px` hamburger-bar radius noted on index.html (low materiality, sitewide); Lato flagged again (intentional, documented sitewide choice, not resume-specific).

## Overall Impression

The visual system holds up, but the page disagrees with its own site about who the candidate is — and that single fact undermines everything else on the page, no matter how well-typeset it is.

## What's Working

- The shared typographic system (`.resume-block-title`, `.resume-item-title` at 700 weight) gives the Experience section a clean, editorial rhythm most resume pages lack.
- The `--space-*` scale keeps vertical rhythm disciplined even where content is dense.
- The ITHAKA entry's sub-headed structure (Accessibility / IA / User Research) proves the progressive-grouping pattern is available — just not applied consistently elsewhere.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Positioning copy directly contradicts the site's own stated identity, in three places on this page alone.**
- **Location:** meta description (`resume.html:8`), `.article-subtitle` (`resume.html:117`: "Senior Experience Designer"), and the summary paragraph (`resume.html:132`: "15+ years").
- **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md's own product principle states "resume and case studies must stay factually consistent with each other," and now specifically targets "principal-level roles" / "20 years." index.html's hero already says "Twenty years." A recruiter reading both pages together sees a candidate whose own materials disagree about seniority and tenure — independently confirmed by both the design-review and detector-evidence passes.
- **Fix:** Update all three locations to match the current positioning (principal-level framing, 20 years) — they currently repeat the same stale claim three times in one file.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] The job-title line has no CSS rule and zero visual hierarchy.**
- **Location:** `&lt;p class="article-subtitle"&gt;` (`resume.html:117`) — confirmed zero matching rules in `stylesgem.css` (a legacy, superseded stylesheet still has one, suggesting this was dropped during the template refresh).
- **Why it matters:** The job title is the fastest seniority signal on the page, for an audience PRODUCT.md says is "scanning for seniority signals" — and it currently renders with *less* visual weight than a metadata label.
- **Fix:** Add a `.article-subtitle` rule using the system's headline weight/scale so the title outranks body copy.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

**[P1] Footer contact/social links contradict the real contact info given at the top of the same page.**
- **Location:** footer (`resume.html:409-413`): "© 2025 ION Portfolio," `mailto:hello@ion.design` (a different, apparently-placeholder address from the real `atomaton@gmail.com` used in the header), and dead `href="#"` links for LinkedIn/Dribbble even though a real LinkedIn URL already exists in the header.
- **Why it matters:** This is a factual inconsistency a recruiter could act on incorrectly (emailing the wrong address), sitting at the bottom of the page — the last thing read, per the peak-end rule.
- **Fix:** Point the footer email/LinkedIn links at the real, correct values already used in the page header; this is a shared nav/footer block, so fixing it once likely fixes it sitewide.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Broken internal link to a missing page.**

- **Why it matters:** A dead nav link is a small but real credibility hit on a page meant to demonstrate craft; it's in the shared "DO NOT EDIT" nav block, so it's likely broken sitewide, not just here.

- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Dense, ungrouped content works against fast scanning.**
- **Location:** Core Competencies (22 items) and Technical Skills (18 items), each one flat `&lt;ul&gt;`; the contact line and 12 date spans confirmed uppercased by the detector.
- **Why it matters:** Fails the chunking guideline (≤4 items/group) by 4-5×, and the ITHAKA entry alone runs ~23 bullets before any subheading breaks it up — a time-pressed recruiter following peak-end behavior won't reach the strongest quantified wins (the 40%/50%+ metrics) buried mid-list.
- **Fix:** Split each skills list into 3-4 labeled subgroups; curate the ITHAKA entry down to its outcome-bearing bullets.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`
